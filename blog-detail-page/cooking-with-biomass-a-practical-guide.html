Cooking with Biomass: A Practical Guide for Commercial Kitchens

Biomass — firewood, agricultural residue and similar fuels — remains one of the most widely available cooking fuels in India. For many commercial kitchens, it is not a compromise but a practical choice driven by availability and cost.

The difference between a good and a poor biomass setup usually comes down to the stove itself. A well-built combustion chamber with proper airflow burns fuel more completely, gives stronger and more consistent heat, and produces less smoke than an open fire.

Features to look for include a lined fire-box, a grate that separates ash from fuel, controllable air supply, and a stable surface for large vessels. Forced-air blowers are a common addition in commercial units because they help the fire respond quickly when cooking loads change.
