Vande Industries builds durable, sustainable equipment from Badli, Delhi — shaped by the real needs of the people who use it.

Who We Are

About Vande Industries

Vande Industries is a product-focused company based in Badli, Delhi. We build practical, durable equipment for commercial and community needs, with our biomass stove range leading the way.

We believe good products come from listening first. Instead of chasing trends, we focus on quality fabrication, practical innovation and solutions that respect both the customer's budget and the environment.

Our Vision

To be a trusted Indian name for practical, sustainable equipment — products that people rely on every day because they simply work.

Our Mission

To design and build quality products around real customer needs — combining honest fabrication, practical innovation and a sustainable approach in everything we make.

Our Approach

How we build, in three chapters

01

Understand the Real Need

We start with how kitchens and businesses actually work — the vessels they use, the fuel they can get, the hours they run.

02

Engineer for Daily Use

We design for strength, airflow and easy handling, then fabricate with materials and welds we would trust ourselves.

03

Keep It Serviceable

Our products are built to be cleaned, repaired and kept in service locally — because downtime costs our customers money.

Quality & Innovation

Quality for us is tangible: steel that holds its shape, welds that hold their line, doors and latches that work smoothly after months of use. Innovation for us is just as practical — better airflow, easier cleaning, steadier vessels. Small improvements that matter every single day.

Sustainable Focus

Our products are designed around biomass — fuels like firewood and agricultural residue that are locally available and renewable. A well-engineered biomass stove uses fuel more completely than an open fire, which means less waste and a more practical path to everyday sustainability.
