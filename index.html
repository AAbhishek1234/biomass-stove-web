About Vande Industries

A product-focused company that builds for real use

Vande Industries is a Delhi-based company focused on building practical, durable products for everyday commercial needs. We work closely with the people who use our equipment, and we let real kitchen requirements — not assumptions — shape what we make.

Our approach is simple: understand the need, engineer a quality solution, and keep it sustainable and serviceable for the long run.
